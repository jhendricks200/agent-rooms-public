Reference: the Codex quality bar

These frames are why Signal lab looks the way it does. John supplied them as the standard to meet: calm neutral surfaces, a three-pane layout, quiet chips and badges, restrained accent color, hairline dividers, an elevated floating composer, and an environment rail that accumulates rows as the session comes alive.

Two specific cues were lifted deliberately. The composer had to float as its own layer rather than sit flat in the page, and the environment rail's habit of gaining rows over time became the room's live rail.
